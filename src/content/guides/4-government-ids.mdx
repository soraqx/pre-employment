## The Big Three

Many employers in the Philippines ask for your **SSS**, **PhilHealth**, and **Pag-IBIG** membership details before onboarding. Initial registration can now begin online, which iz beri convenient I would say.

Use only official government websites, keep your reference numbers, and never pay strangers who promise to “speed up” a registration. Marami kasing ganito, yung "nag-aasist" raw pero those are forged documents.

## SSS

The **Social Security System (SSS)** provides social insurance benefits for private-sector workers and other covered members.

For an initial application:

1. Open the official SSS registration page.
2. Enter your personal information exactly as it appears on your birth certificate.
3. Save the confirmation email and generated reference or SS number.
4. Follow any instructions for submitting supporting documents and updating your membership status.

## PhilHealth

The **Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth)** administers the country's national health insurance program.

Prepare your personal information and supporting identity documents before starting. After registration, keep a record of your **PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)** and any membership form or confirmation provided.

Your employer may ask you to submit or update your member data, so review every detail carefully.

## Pag-IBIG Fund

The **Pag-IBIG Fund**, also known as the Home Development Mutual Fund, provides savings and housing-related benefits to eligible members.

Online registration generally asks for your personal details, contact information, employment information, and valid supporting records. Save your **Registration Tracking Number (RTN)** while waiting for or verifying your permanent Pag-IBIG Membership ID number.

**Official website:** [https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/]

## Keep Your Numbers Cozy and Safe

- 🐾 Save confirmation pages and reference numbers in a secure folder.
- 🐾 Check that your name, birth date, and other details match across all registrations.
- 🐾 Never post membership numbers or identity documents publicly.
- 🐾 Confirm with HR which forms or screenshots they actually require.

Online procedures can change, so treat these steps as a preparation guide and follow the latest instructions shown on each official government website.
