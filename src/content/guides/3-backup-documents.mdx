## Build Your Backup Document Folder

Primary IDs are usually the first choice for identity verification, but employers and government offices may request supporting records when an ID is unavailable, expired, or still being processed.

Prepare these common backup documents:

- 🐾 **PSA Birth Certificate** — Confirms your legal name, birth date, birthplace, and parentage.
- 🐾 **Baptismal Certificate** — May serve as a supporting record when an organization allows secondary documents.
- 🐾 **Voter's Certificate or Voter's ID** — Can help confirm your identity and registered address, depending on the requesting organization's rules.

## Why These Backups Matter

Supporting documents help connect the details across your application, school records, government registrations, and IDs. They can be especially useful when:

- Your primary government ID has not arrived yet.
- Your records use slightly different names or spellings.
- An office requires two or more identity documents.
- You need to correct or verify personal information.

These documents do not automatically replace every primary ID. The employer or agency decides which combinations it accepts, so check its official requirements first.

## Dochi's Document-Care Routine

Keep originals in a dry, secure folder. Prepare readable photocopies and password-protected digital scans, but share them only through trusted channels. Cover or remove information that the recipient does not legitimately need.

Before submitting anything, make sure every copy is complete, legible, and matches the information on your application.
