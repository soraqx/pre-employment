## Build Your Backup Document Folder

Primary IDs are usually the first choice for identity verification, but employers and government offices may request supporting records when an ID is unavailable, expired, or still being processed.

You might wanna prep these common documents:

- 🐾 **PSA Birth Certificate** — Confirms your legal name, birth date, birthplace, and parentage.
- 🐾 **Baptismal Certificate** — May serve as a supporting record when an organization allows secondary documents.
- 🐾 **Voter's Certificate or Voter's ID** — Can help confirm your identity and registered address, depende rin sa recruiter.

## Why These Backups Matter

These documents do not automatically replace every primary ID. The employer or agency decides which combinations it accepts, so check its official requirements first. Pero these requirements will be available after passing interviews and stuff pa sa company.

## Special mention: FTJS (First Time Job Seeker) Certificate

This document can be retrieved from your local barangay hall. Usual requirements include Certificate of Graduation, Diploma, or TOR, and a valid ID.

## Dochi's Document-Care Routine

Keep originals in a dry, secure folder. Prepare readable photocopies (I did at least 3 copies of each).

Before submitting anything, make sure every copy is complete, legible, and matches the information on your application.
