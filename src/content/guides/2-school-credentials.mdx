## Your Academic Paper Trail

Some employers ask for school credentials to confirm your educational background. Requirements vary, so always check the employer's list before requesting or submitting documents.

Typical documents include:

- **Transcript of Records (TOR):** A detailed record of your subjects, grades, and completed academic units.
- **Diploma:** Proof that your school officially awarded your degree or program credential.
- **Certificate of Graduation:** A temporary or supporting certification that confirms you completed your program, especially when the diploma is not yet available.

You may also be asked for a certified true copy, a school-sealed envelope, or a document marked **For Employment Purposes**. Ask the recruiter which format they accept before paying any processing fees.

## A Gentle Cat Tap

🐾 **Request your credentials early.** School registrars may need several working days—or longer during graduation and enrollment seasons—to process requests.

Give yourself a comfortable buffer. Check the registrar's office hours, requirements, fees, accepted payment methods, and whether delivery or online requests are available.

## Before You Leave the Registrar

- Confirm that your full name and graduation details are correct.
- Keep the official receipt or request reference number.
- Ask when and how the documents can be claimed.
- Store originals safely and prepare clear photocopies or scans.
- Do not open a sealed envelope if the employer requires it to remain sealed.

A little early preparation means fewer frantic paw-steps later.
