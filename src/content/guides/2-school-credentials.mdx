## Your Academic Papers Matter

Some employers ask for school credentials to confirm your educational background. Iba-iba requirements ng company, and you'll know this once they want to hire u na, not early on the hiring process.

Typical documents include:

- **Transcript of Records (TOR):** Basta TOR yk what this is mwa
- **Diploma:** Dragon warrior diploma no more
- **Certificate of Graduation:** Temp certification if TOR and Diploma isn't available. Delays sa TOR and Diploma are caused by delayed Special Order Number from CHED.

You may also be asked for a certified true copy, a school-sealed envelope, or a document marked **For Employment Purposes**. Depende sa recruiterr.

I think that's all for this? Keep it tidy lang and organized after getting it. <33
