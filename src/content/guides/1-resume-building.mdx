## Meet the ATS


An **Applicant Tracking System**, or ATS, is software that helps employers collect, organize, and review job applications. With the employment process ngayon, almost every resume goes through ATS before it reaches the HR.

Making an ATS-friendly resume just means u need to be direct with your skills and achievements.

## Formatting Rules

Dapat malinis, yung nababasa ng computer and kaya i-scan ng recruiter. 

- Use familiar headings such as **Work Experience**, **Education**, and **Skills**.
- Choose a readable font and keep the body text at a comfortable size. I just used Arial lol
- Use a simple, single-column layout whenever possible.
- Avoid complex tables, text boxes, charts, and decorative graphics.
- Use standard bullet points instead of icons to describe your achievements.
- Keep dates and job titles formatted consistently.
- Put important information in the main document, not in headers or footers.

## Keywords

Keywords help the ATS connect your resume with the job description. Read the posting carefully and notice the skills, tools, qualifications, and responsibilities it repeats. Keywords can be like "leadership", "communication", "management" etc. Basta skills needed by the majority of job postings in your field.

- Use the employer's exact wording when it honestly matches your experience.
- Include both the full term and abbreviation when helpful, such as **Customer Relationship Management (CRM)**.
- Add keywords naturally inside your experience and skills sections.
- Never copy every keyword or claim a skill you do not have. May limits ang pamemeke, ASHDHASDH

Your resume should still sound like you. The goal is clarity, not keyword stuffing.
